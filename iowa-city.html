A Personalized Approach to Microneedling

At the fictional Luma Skin Studio in Iowa City, our goal is to make professional skin care feel comfortable and approachable. Our microneedling appointments are designed around each client's individual cosmetic goals.

Microneedling is a cosmetic procedure that uses very small needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin. People may seek the treatment because they want to improve the appearance of their skin texture or address other cosmetic skin concerns.

What an Appointment Looks Like

At Luma Skin Studio, a fictional appointment begins with a consultation. A member of our fictional aesthetics team discusses the client's goals, previous skin-care experiences, and what they hope to achieve.

We believe clients should understand the treatment before making a decision. Our team explains the process, expected aftercare, and answers questions before beginning a treatment.
