# A/B Test Plan

## Page and Element Being Tested

The A/B test will be conducted on the Luma Skin Studio homepage (index.html). The element being tested is the call-to-action button in the opening section of the homepage.

Version A: "Explore Microneedling"

Version B: "Learn About Microneedling"

The wording of the button will be the only element changed between the two versions.
## Hypothesis and Reasoning

I predict that Version B, "Learn About Microneedling," will make visitors more likely to click the button than Version A. I think Version B is more clear about what the visitor will see after clicking the button. This could make visitors more comfortable clicking it to learn more about microneedling.
## Model, Persona, and Test Procedure

“I will use ChatGPT GPT-5.6 Sol with the same settings for every trial.” Each trial will be completed in a new conversation so the AI visitor has no memory of previous trials.

The persona will be a potential client in Iowa City who is interested in microneedling and wants to learn more about the treatment before deciding whether to continue exploring the business's website.

Exact prompt:

"You are a potential client in Iowa City who is interested in microneedling. Read the webpage provided. Based only on what you see on the page, would you click the main call-to-action button to learn more about microneedling? Answer Yes or No, then give one sentence explaining your reasoning."

Before each trial, I will randomly assign the visitor to Version A or Version B. I will record the assigned version and the visitor's answer for every trial.
## Primary Outcome

The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says "Yes" or "No" to clicking the main call-to-action button. I will measure the percentage of "Yes" responses for Version A and Version B.

## Sample Size and Stopping Rule

I will complete 100 trials total, with 50 trials for Version A and 50 trials for Version B. I will complete all 100 trials before looking at the final results or deciding which version performed better. I expect to complete all trials by November 15, 2026.
## Comparison and Hypothesis Support

I will compare the percentage of "Yes" responses between Version A and Version B. Version B will support my hypothesis if it receives a higher percentage of "Yes" responses than Version A. I will also analyze whether the difference between the two versions could be due to chance.
## SEO Protection

Version B will not compete with Version A in search results. The Version B page will use a noindex tag and will not be included in the sitemap.
